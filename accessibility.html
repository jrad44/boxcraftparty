Accessibility

Box Craft Party · Last reviewed: November 22, 2026

We want everyone to be able to learn about and enjoy Box Craft Party, including people with disabilities. We aim for this website to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA.

What we do

  • Text alternatives for every image, and clear headings for screen readers.
  • Pages that work with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a "skip to content" link.
  • Readable color contrast, text that can be resized, and layouts that work on phones and tablets.
  • Light and dark color schemes that follow your device settings.
  • No auto-playing audio or video, and no flashing content on this website.

In the game itself, buttons are large, every action gives sound and visual feedback, and sound and music can be turned off. The game is very visual and touch-based, so some parts may be difficult for people who are blind or have limited motor control. We welcome suggestions.

Tell us about a problem
