Box Craft Party!

A cozy crafting game for kids. Paint a cardboard box, cut out paper shapes, squeeze on the glue and stick on googly eyes, pom-poms and holiday decorations.

Coming soon: App Store Coming soon: Google Play

What kids do

🖌️
Paint & wrapFinger-paint, rainbow brushes, stamps, or gift-wrap a whole side.
✂️
Cut paperDraw any shape to cut it out, or let the scissors cut a heart, star or tree.
🧴
Glue & glitterGlue first, then stick things on. No glue? It falls off with a boing!
👀
DecorateGoogly eyes that wobble when you tilt, pom-poms, gems, ornaments and stickers.
🎀
Trim the edgesTwinkle lights, tinsel, ribbon, snow and more.
🖼️
My ShelfEvery finished box is saved to come back to later.

Made for phones and tablets

Big, friendly buttons for little fingers, on iPhone, iPad and Android.

  • Plays sideways or held upright on tablets
  • Works offline, so it's great for car rides
  • Gentle on the battery: it slows down when nobody's touching it

For parents

  • No accounts, no chat, no in-app purchases. Nothing to sign up for.
  • Your child's creations stay on your device. We don't collect personal information.
  • Kid-safe ads keep the app free. A short ad now and then, only after a finished box goes on the shelf, never while crafting. Ads are set up for children: no personalized ads, no tracking, general-audience content only. There's also an optional video kids can choose to watch for bonus stickers.
  • Grown-ups area behind a grown-up question, with the privacy policy and a button to delete saved crafts.
  • Works offline, on phones and tablets (iPhone, iPad, Android).
