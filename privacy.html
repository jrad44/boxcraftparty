Privacy Policy

Box Craft Party · Effective date: November 22, 2026

The short version

  • Box Craft Party is a crafting game for children. It has no accounts, no sign-up, no chat, no in-app purchases and no social features.
  • We (the developer) do not collect, see, store or share any personal information about your child or you. We do not run any servers for the game.
  • Everything your child makes is saved only on your device.
  • The free Android and iPhone/iPad apps show ads from Google AdMob. Those ads are set up for children: no personalized or interest-based ads, no tracking across apps, and general-audience (G-rated) content only. The web version has no ads.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your child's creations (pictures of the decorated boxes and the paper shapes they cut), plus simple settings such as sound on/off and the chosen room. This is stored in the app's private storage on your device. It is never uploaded to us or anyone else. You can delete it at any time from the Grown-ups area (the 👪 button, behind a grown-up question) or by uninstalling the app.

The game can use the device's motion sensor so the googly eyes wobble when the device is tilted. This happens on the device only; motion data is not stored or sent anywhere.

Age signal. Where state law requires it (for example California's Digital Age Assurance Act), the app asks your device's operating system or app store (Apple or Google Play) for the user's age range when it starts. Apple or Google may ask you to share it. We use the answer only on the device, for that session: it is never saved or sent to us or anyone else, and it does not change how the game works, because every player gets the same child-safe experience and ad settings regardless of age.

3. Advertising (Android and iOS apps only)

To keep the game free, the apps show a short full-screen ad now and then (about one every 8 to 10 minutes of play, at most six per session), and only at a natural pause after a finished box has been saved to the shelf. Ads never appear while a child is crafting or looking at their shelf. There is also an optional video (at most three a day) a child may choose to watch to open the week’s bonus sticker pack for the day and keep one sticker from it. Which stickers a child has collected is stored only on the device. There are no banner ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). We configure every ad request as follows:

  • Child-directed: requests are tagged under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) as directed to children ("tag for child-directed treatment").
  • Under the age of consent: requests are tagged for users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland as being under the age of consent ("tag for under age of consent").
  • No personalized ads: because of these settings, Google disables interest-based advertising and remarketing for the game.
  • G-rated ads only: the maximum ad content rating is set to "G" (general audiences).
  • No advertising ID: the Android app removes access to the Android Advertising ID, and the iOS app never asks for permission to track (it does not use the IDFA).

To show ads, count views and prevent fraud, Google's software in the app may process limited technical information, such as the device's IP address (used for approximate, city-level location), device model, operating system and app version, and whether an ad was viewed or tapped. Google processes this information under its own policies and the child-directed restrictions described above; we do not receive information that identifies your child. Learn more: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses information from apps that use its services · AdMob child-directed settings.

Tapping an ad may open an app store or a website outside the game. We ask Google to show only family-appropriate ads, but we do not choose individual ads.

4. Ads and products that are never shown

We tell our ad provider that the game is directed to children, limit ads to general-audience content, and block ad categories that are not suitable for children, including alcohol, tobacco and vaping, firearms and weapons, gambling and lotteries, dating, and other sensitive categories, consistent with California's law on advertising to minors (Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 22580).

5. What we do NOT do

  • We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, photos, voice recordings, location or contacts.
  • We do not use analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of our own.
  • We do not sell or share personal information, and we do not use it for targeted advertising.
  • The game has no chat, no way for children to post anything publicly, and no way to contact other people.
  • Links outside the game (such as the links in this policy) are only in the Grown-ups area, behind a question a young child cannot answer.

6. Children's privacy (COPPA) and parental rights

The game is designed for children, and we comply with COPPA. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because we hold no information about your child, there is nothing for us to review, change or delete, but you may contact us at any time with questions or requests, and you can delete everything the game has stored using "Delete crafts" in the Grown-ups area or by uninstalling the app. If you believe information about your child has been collected through the game, contact us and we will help, including by passing your request to Google where it concerns ads.

7. Where the game is offered

The Box Craft Party apps are not offered in the European Union, Iceland, Liechtenstein, Norway, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, and are not available in the app stores there. Our website collects no personal data from anyone (see section 10). If you contact us from one of those places, we handle your message only to reply and will honor any request to see or delete it.

8. California residents and other U.S. states

  • No selling or sharing: we do not sell or share personal information, including that of consumers under 16, and we do not use personal information for cross-context behavioral (targeted) advertising.
  • "Shine the Light" (Cal. Civ. Code § 1798.83): we do not disclose personal information to third parties for their own direct-marketing purposes.
  • Your requests: you can ask us what information we hold (none, from us) or ask us to delete or correct information by emailing or writing to us at the contact details above. We will respond within 45 days and will not treat you differently for asking.
  • Removing content (Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 22581): the game has no way to post content publicly. Everything a child makes stays on the device and can be deleted with "Delete crafts" in the Grown-ups area.

9. Do Not Track and tracking by others

Some browsers and devices can send a "Do Not Track" signal. The game and our website do not track users over time or across other websites or apps, so there is nothing for the signal to switch off and we treat every user the same way. We do not allow third parties to collect personal information about children's activity across other sites and apps for advertising: our ad provider is configured for child-directed, non-personalized ads only and may use limited technical information solely to show and measure ads and prevent fraud (see section 3).

10. Our website

Our website, boxcraftparty.com, has no accounts, ads, cookies or analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses) for security and reliability under GitHub's privacy statement. If you email us, we use your message and email address only to reply, and we delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

11. Data retention

We do not collect or keep personal information from children, so we have nothing to retain. On your device, creations and settings stay in the app's private storage until you delete them ("Delete crafts" in the Grown-ups area) or uninstall the app. Our ad provider keeps the limited technical information described in section 3 only as long as needed to show and measure ads and prevent fraud, under its own retention policies; we never receive it. Emails from parents are kept only as long as needed to answer them and then deleted, generally within one year.

12. How we protect information

We keep a written information security program for the game, coordinated by Jared Lewis. It includes: no servers, accounts or databases that hold children's information; storing creations only in the app's private, sandboxed storage on the device; all game content built into the app (the app cannot browse the web); configuring every ad request as child-directed; reviewing third-party software before adding it and keeping it updated; protecting our developer and store accounts with strong passwords and two-factor authentication; and reviewing these safeguards at least once a year and whenever the app changes.

13. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this policy and its effective date, and post the new version at boxcraftparty.com/privacy.html and in the app. If a change would mean collecting personal information from children, we will obtain verifiable parental consent first, as required by law.
